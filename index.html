I am a PhD student in cosmology at the Centre de Physique des Particules de Marseille (CPPM, Aix-Marseille Université, CNRS/IN2P3), supervised by Alice Pisani. I work on cosmic voids as a cosmological probe, and on what is needed to use them in photometric galaxy surveys, mainly the Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST) of the Vera C. Rubin Observatory.

I am a member of LSST DESC, where I lead the void group, of the Euclid Consortium and of DESI. My thesis, Cosmic Voids in Photometric Surveys: Systematics of the Void Size Function, will be defended in September 2027.
