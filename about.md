## About me

<p>I am Pierre Boccard, a PhD student in observational cosmology at the <a href="https://www.cppm.in2p3.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Centre de Physique des Particules de Marseille (CPPM, CNRS / IN2P3)</a>, within <a href="https://www.univ-amu.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Aix-Marseille Université</a>. I work under the supervision of <a href="https://www.cppm.in2p3.fr/spip/spip.php?article2099&amp;lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Alice Pisani</a> on the imprint of photometric-redshift systematics on the cosmic void abundance. I am a member of the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC), where I lead the void group, of the Euclid Consortium and of DESI.</p>

<p>My PhD is titled <strong>"Cosmic Voids in Photometric Surveys: Systematics of the Void Size Function"</strong>, and I will defend it in September 2027. Cosmic voids — the vast, underdense regions of the universe — are exquisite cosmological probes, but the noisy distance estimates of photometric surveys distort their statistics. My work aims to model these effects and to build calibration strategies that let Rubin/LSST and Euclid exploit voids as a competitive cosmological probe.</p>

<p>Before joining CPPM, I obtained a Master in Astrophysics at the <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Swiss Federal Institute of Technology (EPFL)</a>. I carried out my Master Thesis at the University of Chicago with Prof. Joshua Frieman and Dr. Anowar Shajib, measuring the dark-energy equation of state from the compound strong-lens system DES J0408−5354 with <em>Lenstronomy</em>. Earlier I worked at LASTRO (EPFL) with Jean-Paul Kneib on the void–galaxy cross-correlation as a redshift-space-distortion probe.</p>

<p>I was raised in France by a Croatian mother and a French father — both scientists — and grew up between French, Croatian and English. Outside the lab I shoot 35 mm film, play music, and chase long-distance running goals along the calanques of Marseille.</p>

My GitHub is [PierreBoccard](https://github.com/PierreBoccard).
