This project is my PhD work on finding and characterizing **cosmic voids** — the giant, mostly empty regions between galaxy filaments — using large photometric galaxy surveys.

* Goal: build a reliable void catalog from photometric redshifts and use it to probe cosmology.
* Methods: density-field reconstruction, void-finding algorithms, and validation with realistic mock catalogs.
* Outputs: void statistics (sizes, shapes, profiles) and visualizations that make the cosmic web intuitive.

### Overview

Cosmic voids are enormous bubbles in the universe where galaxies are sparse. Because they are so empty, they are clean laboratories to test gravity and the expansion history of the universe. Photometric surveys give us vast numbers of galaxies, but with fuzzier distances — this project focuses on extracting robust voids from that imperfect but powerful data.
